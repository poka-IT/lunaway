Changes (app/tool/fonts/figtree_lunaway.py)
  - the lowercase l and its accented forms (l, lacute, lcaron, lcommaaccent,
    lslash) take the tailed glyphs of the upstream ss02 set by default;
  - U+2009 THIN SPACE and U+202F NARROW NO-BREAK SPACE map to a new empty
    glyph half as wide as the space at every weight;
  - family renamed Figtree Lunaway (PostScript FigtreeLunaway), with a note
    of these changes in the name table.

Rebuild
  app/tool/fonts/build.sh downloads the two upstream files above, checks
  their SHA-256, derives FigtreeLunaway-Variable.ttf and checks the result
  against app/tool/fonts/fonts.sha256 (two runs give the same bytes).
