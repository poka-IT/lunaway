--- main

The app

A map made for the night

Every place says first whether you can sleep there. Then come the services, the height limit, the coordinates and the sources.

Our principles

Free, open and discreet

  • {icon:hand-heart}Free, with no ads

    Every feature is free. The app shows no advertising, and we sell no data.

  • {icon:shield-check}No trackers

    No audience measurement, no advertising SDK, no third-party crash reporting. The app connects only to our own servers, in Germany. The details are in the privacy policy.

  • {icon:cloud-slash}Works without a network

    The places of France are stored on your device: search, filters and place pages answer where there is no signal. A place's photos and reviews load the first time you open it, then stay on the device.

  • {icon:seal-check}Sources you can see

    Places come from OpenStreetMap, French public datasets and the Lunaway community. Every place shows where each piece of information comes from, and under which licence.

  • {icon:users}A community without email

    Ratings, reviews, photos, "still there?" confirmations and new places come from the people who travel. The account asks for no email, no phone number and no password.

  • {icon:path}Directions for your vehicle

    In preparation: guidance that takes the height, width, length and weight of your vehicle into account, from OpenStreetMap and IGN data. Until then, Lunaway hands directions to the navigation app of your choice.
