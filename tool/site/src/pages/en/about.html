The project

Lunaway helps you find where to stop with a motorhome or a van: service areas, car parks, campsites, farm stays, spots in nature, water and dump points. France is covered in detail, and the app is designed for the whole world, in French and English.

The app is free, with no ads and no trackers, and we sell no data. It is published by AxiomTeam, and its code is free software.

It is made of a Flutter app (Android and web today, iPhone and computers next), an API written in Rust on PostgreSQL and PostGIS, and servers of our own, at Hetzner in Germany, which also serve the basemap. Each place gathers what several sources say about the same spot, and keeps, for each piece of information, the source that supplied it.

Lunaway imports no proprietary database of places, reviews or photos: their terms forbid reuse, and their reviews and photos belong to the people who wrote or took them. A producer who wishes to offer its data under a licence can write to us.

Contribute

  • In the app: rate a place, confirm it is "still there", report a problem, write a review, add a photo or a place. A new account can rate, confirm and report; reviews and photos open after three days and three confirmed places, adding places after two weeks and ten published contributions. These steps hold back spam without asking for an email.
  • In OpenStreetMap: a correction made on openstreetmap.org reaches Lunaway with the daily import, and helps every free map.
  • In the code: report a bug or suggest an improvement on GitHub.

Source code

All of Lunaway's code, the app, the server, the infrastructure and this site, is at github.com/poka-IT/lunaway. For a question, write to contact@lunaway.net.
