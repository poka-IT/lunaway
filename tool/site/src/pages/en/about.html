About Lunaway

A free map of places to stop with a motorhome or a van, built on open data and on its own community.

The project

Lunaway helps you find where to stop with a motorhome or a van: service areas, car parks, campsites, farm stays, spots in the wild, water and dump points. The places cover France and twenty other European countries, and the app speaks French, English, German, Spanish, Italian and Dutch.

The app is free, with no ads and no trackers, and we sell no data. It is published by AxiomTeam, and its code is free software.

It is made of a Flutter app (Android, iPhone and iPad, macOS, Windows and the web), an API written in Rust on PostgreSQL and PostGIS, and servers of our own, at Hetzner in Germany, which also serve the basemap, compute the routes and run the address search and the translation of reviews. Each place gathers what several sources say about the same spot, and keeps, for each piece of information, the source that supplied it.

Sources and attributions

Lunaway relies on open data, on its community's contributions and on an external community source, under a written agreement. Every place page shows its sources, with their licence, the original identifier and the date of collection.

Lunaway imports a proprietary database of places, reviews or photos only under a written agreement with its producer. The external community source is the only one in that case; its data joins neither the open places database nor its exports. A producer who wishes to offer its data under a licence can write to us.

Contribute

  • In the app: rate a place, confirm it is "still there", report a problem, write a review, add a photo or a place, report a problem on the road during guidance. A new account can rate, confirm and report; reviews and photos open after three days and three confirmed places, adding places after two weeks and ten published contributions. These steps hold back spam without asking for an e-mail address.
  • In OpenStreetMap: a correction made on openstreetmap.org reaches Lunaway with the next import, and helps every free map.
  • In the code: report a bug or suggest an improvement on GitHub.

Source code
