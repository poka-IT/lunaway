Delete your Lunaway account

Lunaway, the map app for motorhomes and vans published by AxiomTeam, lets you delete your account and its data at any time, in two ways.

--- main

1. In the app

Open Profile, then Delete my account under Your account. The app shows what will be deleted and what will stay; tap Continue, tick "I understand this is final", then tap Delete the account. The app needs an internet connection. The deletion is immediate, and the contributions still waiting to be sent are erased from the device.

2. On this page, with your recovery code

If you no longer have the device, use the recovery code the app gave you when you created your recovery card.

To protect accounts, our server accepts 5 recovery code attempts an hour from the same connection. Try again in 60 minutes.

The server does not accept it as a recovery code. Compare it with your card, character by character.

The request did not go through

The server did not answer, or answered with an error, and nothing says whether the account was deleted. Try again in a few minutes: if the account no longer exists, this page will tell you. If the problem persists, write to contact@lunaway.net.

Without the device or the code

The account is linked to no e-mail address and no phone number: without the device or the recovery code, nothing can prove that an account is yours. Write to us anyway at contact@lunaway.net, and we will look for a solution with you.

What is deleted

At once, in a single operation:

  • the account itself: pseudonym, trust level, activity dates, and the sponsorships given or received;
  • your devices' keys, all your sessions and your recovery code;
  • your favourite lists stored on the server, with the addresses and points you had saved in them;
  • the authors you had muted, and the mutes aimed at your account;
  • all your photos, files included;
  • your ratings without text, and those of your reviews that were not published;
  • your "Still there?" answers about shops and services;
  • your problem reports about places, your road reports, and your reports of reviews, photos or places;
  • the content and position of your new places or edits that were not applied (pending, rejected or withdrawn);
  • the technical keys of your contributions.

On the device, your favourites stay, detached from the account, and the account's key is erased.

What stays, without your name

Some contributions are public under an open licence and help other travellers. They stay, detached from your account:

  • your published reviews with a text (text, rating, date of visit, vehicle), shown as coming from a "deleted account", with their translations;
  • your "Still there?" confirmations of places, without the note you attached, until they are two years old;
  • your new places or vending machines and your edits already applied, with their content and position: they are part of the open places database (ODbL);
  • the road events your reports led to, which never carried a name.

For a review to go too, delete it in the app before deleting your account.

An account banned for abuse that is then deleted also loses its confirmations; the hashes of its keys are kept for two years, so that it cannot come straight back.

How soon

The deletion is immediate in our database. The backups of the database and of the photos (unencrypted for 7 nights on the main server, encrypted elsewhere) keep a copy for about 30 days, then erase it (details in the privacy policy); they serve only to bring the service back after a failure, and a backup brought back into service does not bring a deleted account back: the deletion, noted in a separate journal (the account's identifier and the time, 45 days at most, copied every night with the backups), is applied again. Only the loss of the server's data disk itself would bring back the deletions made after the last copy collected away from that server (up to about 25 hours).

Delete a single contribution

Without deleting your account, you can delete in the app, from Profile, My contributions, each of your reviews and ratings, each of your photos, confirmations, answers about shops and services and problem reports about places, and each of your new places or edits.

  • A review or a photo is erased for good, with two exceptions that leave a record for the moderators, linked to your account until the account is deleted: a review reported or hidden by moderators loses its text and stops counting, but its rating stays in that record; a photo reported, hidden or still waiting for a moderator loses its files and is no longer shown.
  • A new place or edit not yet applied is withdrawn and will never be applied; its content is erased 30 days later, and an empty line stays in the revision history, linked to your account until the account is deleted. One already applied stays in the places database, without your name.
  • A road report cannot be deleted on its own: it erases itself after 14 days.
