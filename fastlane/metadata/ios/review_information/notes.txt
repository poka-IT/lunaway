Lunaway is a free map of places to stop with a motorhome or a van, in France and twenty other European countries, with turn-by-turn guidance for the vehicle's size. It has no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It speaks French, English, German, Spanish, Italian and Dutch.

Browsing, search and directions need no account. An account is created, without e-mail or password, at the user's first contribution (rating, confirmation, review, photo, report, mute, new place) or favourites sync. It is a key pair kept on the device; a recovery code restores it on another device.

Trust levels: a new account can rate places, confirm them, report problems and sync favourites. Reviews and photos need level 1, and adding a new place needs level 2. To review these features, sign in to the level 2 demo account:
1. Open the Profile tab and tap "Recover my account".
2. Type the recovery code given in the Sign-In Information (password field), then tap "Recover the account".

Account deletion (guideline 5.1.1(v)): Profile, "Your account", "Delete my account", "Continue", tick "I understand this is final", then "Delete the account". The same deletion is available on https://lunaway.net/en/account/delete with the recovery code.

User-generated content (guideline 1.2): texts pass automatic rules (links, contact details, repetition, a word list) and wait for a moderator when they trip them; every review, photo and place has an action to report it to the moderators; a review or photo reported by three trusted accounts is hidden until a moderator decides; users can mute an author from a review or a photo; contact: contact@lunaway.net.

Location is requested "When In Use" only. It shows the user on the map and sorts places by distance. It reaches our server as the start of a route, which the server does not store; rounded to about 5 km for the cheapest fuel around the user, and with a search while the map follows the user; as the position of a new place, which the user sets on the map and confirms; and with a road report the user makes during guidance (closed road, roadworks, narrow passage, low clearance, in France, Spain and the Netherlands), kept 14 days with the account.

Background location and audio (guideline 2.5.4) are used only during guidance that the user starts towards a place: the app follows the vehicle, speaks the instructions and the alerts (a bridge lower than the vehicle, a closure, a danger zone), and stops both when the route ends or is cancelled. The blue location indicator shows meanwhile. To try it, open a place, tap Directions, then "Let's go!".

Speed limits and speed cameras: on the map of a route and during guidance, the app shows the speed limit for the vehicle and what the law of the country allows: fixed cameras with their limit where their positions may be shown, danger zones elsewhere (France by default: a stretch of road, never a camera's position or type). In France a setting, off by default, shows the cameras' positions instead; the sentence under it cites the penalty of the Code de la route (art. R413-15). Nothing shows in Switzerland, Germany, Morocco, Liechtenstein, Monaco, San Marino, the Vatican or a country not reviewed, and nothing on the main map. No police check is reported. The voice has three modes ("Full voice", "Voice: alerts only", "Voice off"); an upcoming zone or camera is announced after a short chime unless the voice is off; the spoken reminder of the speed limit is off by default, and exceeding the limit shows as a warning only (guideline 1.4.4).

Translations run on our own server. Our server passes the text of an address search, with the map centre rounded to about 5 km (the user's rounded position while the map follows the user) and without the user's IP address, to IGN's public geocoding service.

The camera and the photo library open only to add a photo to a place or read the recovery card.

The app talks only to our own servers (api.lunaway.net, tiles.lunaway.net), in production.
