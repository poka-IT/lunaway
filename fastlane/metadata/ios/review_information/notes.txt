Lunaway is a free map of places to stop with a motorhome or a van, in metropolitan France. It has no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchase.

Browsing needs no account. An account is created, without e-mail or password, the first time the user contributes (a rating, a "Still there?" confirmation, a review, a photo, a problem report, a report to the moderators, muting an author, a new place) or chooses to sync favourites. It rests on a key pair kept on the device; a recovery code brings it back on another device.

Trust levels: a new account can rate places, confirm them, report problems and sync favourites. Reviews and photos need level 1, and adding a new place needs level 2. To review these features, sign in to the level 2 demo account:
1. Open the Profile tab and tap "Recover my account".
2. Type the recovery code given in the Sign-In Information (password field), then tap "Recover the account".

Account deletion (guideline 5.1.1(v)): Profile, "Your account", "Delete my account", tick "I understand this is final", then "Delete the account". The same deletion is available on https://lunaway.net/account/delete with the recovery code.

User-generated content (guideline 1.2): texts pass automatic rules (links, contact details, repetition, a word list) and wait for a moderator when they trip them; every review, photo and place has an action to report it to the moderators; a review or photo reported by three trusted accounts is hidden until a moderator decides; users can mute an author from a review or a photo; contact: contact@lunaway.net.

Location is read only while the app is in use, to show the user on the map and sort places by distance. It reaches our servers only as the position of a new place, which the user sets on the map and confirms. The camera and the photo library open only when the user adds a photo to a place or reads the recovery card from a photo.

The app talks only to our own servers (api.lunaway.net, tiles.lunaway.net), which the review uses in production.
