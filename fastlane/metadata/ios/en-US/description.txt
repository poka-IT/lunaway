Lunaway is a map of places to stop with a motorhome or a van: motorhome areas, service areas, car parks, campsites, farm stays, spots in the wild, in France and twenty other European countries.

Free, with no ads and no trackers: every feature is free, offline use included. The code is free software (AGPL) and the places database is open (ODbL).

FIND WHERE TO SLEEP
• The overnight status on every page: allowed, tolerated, daytime only or no overnight stay
• Filters: kind of place, night possible, free, services, minimum rating, open all year or on your dates
• "My vehicle fits": hides places with a height limit lower than your vehicle
• Search for a place, a town or an address
• Overnight and service prices, season, opening hours, photos, reviews and sources on every page

DIRECTIONS AND GUIDANCE FOR YOUR VEHICLE'S SIZE
• Routes computed for the height, width, length and weight of your vehicle and its trailer: bridges that are too low and roads closed to your vehicle's size are left out
• Turn-by-turn guidance inside the app, rerouting when you leave the route, up to five stops
• Voice in three modes: full voice, alerts only, or voice off
• Speed cameras and danger zones as each country's law allows, speed limit for your vehicle
• Roadworks and closures updated during the trip; the route goes around closures
• Avoid tolls, motorways, ferries or unpaved roads

ON THE WAY
Fuel, places to sleep, water and waste disposal, groceries, bakeries, toilets and showers, health, charging, garages: what lies along your route, with the detour and whether it is open when you pass. One tap adds it as a stop.

AROUND YOU
Shops, water and waste disposal, fuel, health, services, restaurants, sights, with their opening hours. Fuel prices at French stations and a list of the cheapest around you.

OFFLINE, FOR FREE
Choose the regions to keep: their places stay on the device and update, over Wi-Fi by default. The list, search, filters, place pages and your favourites work without a network. The basemap downloads by region too.

REVIEWS IN YOUR LANGUAGE
A review written in another language translates with one tap, or automatically if you turn that on. The translation runs on Lunaway's servers: the text is sent to no third-party service.

TAKE PART
• Ratings, reviews and photos; "Still there?" to say whether a place still matches its page
• Report a problem, suggest an edit, add a place
• During guidance, report a closed road, roadworks, a narrow passage or a low clearance (France, Spain, the Netherlands)

AN ACCOUNT WITHOUT E-MAIL
The map and directions need no account. One is created at your first contribution, without e-mail or password, with a pseudonym you can change. A recovery card brings it back on another device. Each contribution, and the whole account, can be deleted from the app.

FAVOURITES AND VEHICLE
Favourites in lists, on the device and with your account if you have one. Vehicle profile: kind, trailer, size, weight, fuel, consumption.

SIX LANGUAGES
French, English, German, Spanish, Italian and Dutch, voice guidance included. Light or dark theme that follows the sun. Layouts for iPhone and iPad.

SOURCES
Open data, contributions from the Lunaway community and an external community source under a written agreement. Every place page shows its sources and their licences.
